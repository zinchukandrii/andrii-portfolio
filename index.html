ENGINEERING APPROACH

What I optimise for.

The goal is not a convincing demo. It is a system whose limits, data boundaries and decisions can be inspected.

  1. 01

    Evidence first

    Claims are grounded in reproducible fixtures, clear sources or explicit test results.

  2. 02

    Fail closed

    Invalid configuration, missing knowledge and unknown outcomes do not silently become success.

  3. 03

    Bounded automation

    Automation is scoped, auditable and kept separate from unverified external action.

  4. 04

    Privacy by default

    Public work uses synthetic fixtures or offline simulations — never employer, client or production data.
